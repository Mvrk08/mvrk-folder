Here are the primary allies of the Punisher (Frank Castle) in Marvel Comics:

Key Logistics & Tech Allies

• Microchip (David Linus Lieberman): Frank's most famous sidekick who built his weapons, managed his high-tech safehouses, and hacked networks until their eventual, violent falling out.
• Henry Russo: The tech-savvy son of Jigsaw who temporarily acted as the Punisher's hacker and logistics partner to make amends for his father's sins.

Superhero & Anti-Hero Cooperators

• Daredevil (Matt Murdock): Though they share a fierce ideological rivalry over killing, they frequently team up against major crime syndicates like the Hand or the Kingpin.
• Spider-Man (Peter Parker): While Spider-Man fundamentally opposes the Punisher's methods, the two have joined forces numerous times to take down shared rogue elements.
• Wolverine (Logan): A frequent ally whose mutant healing factor and gritty outlook align well with Frank's brutal combat style during joint missions.
• Ghost Rider (Johnny Blaze / Danny Ketch): A supernatural ally who shares Frank's absolute dedication to punishing the guilty.

Teammates & Specialized Groups

• The Thunderbolts: A team of anti-heroes and assassins led by Red Hulk, which Frank joined alongside Elektra, Deadpool, and Agent Venom.
• The Marvel Knights: A street-level street alliance formed by Daredevil that included Frank, Moon Knight, Shang-Chi, and Black Widow.
